# DVDStyler

DVDStyler is a cross-platform, free and open-source DVD authoring application. It
lets you create professional-looking video DVDs with attractive, animated
menus — no video-encoding knowledge required. You bring your own video and
photo files; DVDStyler takes care of the menus, chapters, transitions,
subtitles and burning.

* **Home page:** <http://www.dvdstyler.org>
* **Project wiki & documentation:** <https://github.com/ntxalex/DVDStyler/wiki>
* **Source:** <https://github.com/ntxalex/DVDStyler>

---

## Features

* **WYSIWYG menu editor** — build custom DVD menus by placing buttons, images,
  text, shapes and animations on a canvas, with ready-made templates
  (Wedding, Christmas, Birthday, Nature, Travel, Seasons and more).
* **Titleset manager** — organize video titles into chapters and menus, add
  subtitles and multiple audio tracks.
* **Flexible video handling** — copy supported MPEG streams or transcode with
  FFmpeg, with options for scaling, padding, cropping and aspect-ratio control.
* **Slideshow support** — turn photos (with optional music) into DVD slideshows.
* **Audio normalisation** and **burning** to disc (dvd+rw-tools on Linux,
  DiscRecording on macOS).
* **Internationalised** — translated into 30+ languages.
* **Cross-platform** — native builds for Windows, Linux and macOS (including
  Apple Silicon and Retina displays).
